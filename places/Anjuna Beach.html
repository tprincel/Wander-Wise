About Anjuna Beach

Anjuna Beach, a coastal gem in North Goa, is more than just golden sands and azure waters. It's a canvas where history, culture, and modernity blend seamlessly. From its hippie roots to its vibrant present, Anjuna remains a captivating destination for travellers seeking both tranquility and adventure.

In the 1960s, Anjuna was a haven for free-spirited hippies seeking enlightenment and escape. Their legacy lingers in the air, making Anjuna a unique blend of bohemian vibes and beach bliss.

Unlike many Goan beaches, Anjuna's shoreline boasts rugged rocks jutting into the sea. These formations create intimate coves and interesting spots to explore, adding to its distinctive charm.

Unique Features

  • Rocky shoreline with distinctive rock formations
  • Intimate coves perfect for private relaxation
  • Historic hippie legacy from the 1960s
  • Bohemian atmosphere blending tradition with modernity
  • Spectacular sunset views over Arabian Sea

Activities & Attractions

  • Famous Wednesday Flea Market with local crafts and goods
  • Vibrant nightlife with beach parties and clubs
  • Water sports including parasailing and jet skiing
  • Beach shacks serving fresh seafood and drinks
  • Trance parties continuing the hippie music legacy

Visitor Information

  • Best time to visit: October to March
  • Market day: Wednesday Flea Market
  • Nearby attractions: Chapora Fort, Vagator Beach
  • Accommodation: Beach huts, guesthouses, resorts
  • Experience: Perfect blend of relaxation and excitement
