About Ayodhya

In the heart of Uttar Pradesh, along the banks of the sacred Sarayu River, lies the ancient city of Ayodhya. Revered by millions as the birthplace of Lord Rama, Ayodhya is steeped in mythology, history, and spirituality, making it a cherished pilgrimage site and a symbol of India's cultural heritage.

Ayodhya's history is intertwined with the epic Ramayana, one of the most revered texts in Hindu mythology. According to legend, Ayodhya was founded by the legendary king Manu, but it was Lord Rama's divine presence that elevated the city to celestial heights.

As the heir to the throne of Ayodhya, Rama's life journey—from exile to triumphant return—embodies the timeless principles of righteousness, duty, and devotion that continue to inspire humanity across generations.
