About Victoria Memorial

In the heart of Kolkata, where the historic Maidan meets Queens Way, stands the Victoria Memorial - a breathtaking marvel where India's colonial past meets its sovereign future. This architectural masterpiece spans an impressive 64 acres of meticulously curated gardens.

Step through its grand halls and be transported through time as you explore its 25 distinct galleries. Marvel at the world's largest collection of Thomas and William Daniell's works, or lose yourself in rare illustrated Shakespeare volumes and Arabian Nights.

The Royal Gallery beckons with its stunning portraits of Queen Victoria and Prince Albert by renowned artists, while personal artefacts like Victoria's childhood pianoforte offer intimate glimpses into royal life.

Collections & Galleries

  • 25 distinct galleries showcasing diverse historical collections
  • World's largest collection of Thomas and William Daniell's works
  • Rare illustrated volumes of Shakespeare and Arabian Nights
  • Royal Gallery with portraits of Queen Victoria and Prince Albert
  • Personal royal artefacts including Victoria's childhood pianoforte

Historical Significance

  • Indian National Army artefacts and historical documents
  • Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose's personal belongings
  • Rani Jhansi Regiment's iconic uniform display
  • Kolkata Gallery's life-sized diorama of 19th-century Chitpur Road
  • Historical statues transforming gardens into open-air gallery

Visitor Information

  • Annual visitors: 5 million people explore the memorial
  • Collection size: Over 50,000 historical pieces
  • Architectural style: Indo-Saracenic revivalist style
  • Must-see: Light and sound show in evenings
  • Experience: Journey through India's rich cultural tapestry
