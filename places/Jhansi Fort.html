Basic Information

Location

Jhansi, Uttar Pradesh, India

Built By

Raja Bir Singh Ju Deo of Orchha

Area

15 acres (312m x 225m)

About Jhansi Fort

Spanning a vast expanse of 15 acres, the grand fortress commands attention with its dimensions of 312 meters in length and 225 meters in width. The fort was crafted from solid granite, and its walls boast a thickness ranging from 16 to 20 feet, ensuring its resilience through the ages.

Built upon the rugged terrain of Bangra Hill by the visionary Raja Bir Singh Ju Deo of Orchha, the fort draws countless tourists and history enthusiasts to its storied gates each year, standing as a testament to India's rich heritage and the bravery of its people.

Jhansi Fort is most renowned for its association with Rani Lakshmibai and her heroic defense during the 1857 Revolt, making it a symbol of Indian resistance and courage.

Architectural Features

  • Solid granite construction ensuring durability

  • Bangra Hill location providing strategic advantage
  • Ten majestic gates with unique names and histories

The Ten Gates

  • Bhanderi Gate - Rani Lakshmibai's escape route
  • Khandero Gate - steeped in legend and lore
  • Datia Darwaza - connecting to neighboring region
  • Unnao Gate, Jharna Gate - additional access points
  • Laxmi Gate, Sagar Gate - named after deities
  • Orcha Gate, Sainyar Gate, Chand Gate - completing the ten
