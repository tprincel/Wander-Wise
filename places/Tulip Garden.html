TULIP GARDEN

Basic Information

Location

Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir, India

Key Feature

Asia's largest tulip garden with 48 varieties of tulips

Also Known As

Indira Gandhi Memorial Tulip Garden

About the Garden

A vibrant garden showcasing colorful tulips in full bloom every spring. Located at the base of the Zabarwan Mountain is the Indira Gandhi Memorial Tulip Garden, Asia's largest tulip garden and a key attraction in Srinagar. This seven-terraced garden is celebrated for its 48 varieties of tulip flowers, appealing to both adults and children alike.

The garden is also home to a diverse array of other flowers, including daffodils, hyacinths, roses, narcissus, and various ornamental plants. A water channel flowing through the terraces enhances the garden's beauty, providing an idyllic setting for memorable photographs.

Garden Features

  • Seven terraced layout offering stunning views of Dal Lake
  • 48 varieties of tulip flowers in vibrant colors
  • Additional flowers: daffodils, hyacinths, roses, and narcissus
  • Scenic water channels flowing through the terraces
  • Perfect photography spot with breathtaking mountain backdrop

Visitor Information

  • Best time to visit: Spring season (March-April)
  • Blooming period: Tulips bloom for about a month
  • Annual Tulip Festival attracts thousands of visitors
  • Family-friendly destination with something for all ages
  • Ideal for: Nature lovers, photographers, and peace seekers
