MAHABODHI TEMPLE

Basic Information

Location

Bodh Gaya, Bihar, India

Significance

Where Buddha attained enlightenment

UNESCO Status

World Heritage Site since 2002

About Mahabodhi Temple

Bodh Gaya's Mahabodhi Temple stands as an iconic pilgrimage site worldwide for Buddhists. This ancient Buddhist temple marks the sacred spot where Siddhartha Gautama attained enlightenment and became the Buddha.

No matter your interest - be it religion, art, culture or simply curiosity - here you are sure to discover something spectacular. Come find out why a trip to India should include visiting this site at the earliest!

The temple complex represents one of the earliest Buddhist temples built entirely in brick, still standing in India, from the late Gupta period.

Spiritual Significance

  • Site of Buddha's enlightenment under the Bodhi Tree
  • One of the four main Buddhist pilgrimage sites associated with Buddha's life
  • Sacred Bodhi Tree - direct descendant of the original tree
  • Vajrasana (Diamond Throne) - the exact spot of enlightenment
  • Global pilgrimage destination for Buddhists worldwide

Architectural Features

  • 50-meter tall pyramidal tower (shikhara)
  • Built entirely in brick from late Gupta period
  • Four smaller towers at each corner of main temple
  • Intricate stone railings with Buddhist motifs
  • Ancient stupas and votive shrines throughout complex

Visitor Information

  • Open to all visitors regardless of religion
  • Meditation sessions available throughout the day
  • Best time to visit: October to March
  • Nearby attractions: Various monasteries from different Buddhist countries
  • Cultural experience: Witness diverse Buddhist traditions and practices
